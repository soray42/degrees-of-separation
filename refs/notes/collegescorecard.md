# collegescorecard
**Full citation:** U.S. Department of Education. (2026). College Scorecard: Field of Study Data. Office of Postsecondary Education / National Center for Education Statistics. collegescorecard.ed.gov/data.
**DOI / URL:** https://collegescorecard.ed.gov/data/ (Field of Study documentation: https://collegescorecard.ed.gov/files/FieldOfStudyDataDocumentation.pdf)
**Access level:** full text (public data portal and documentation read directly)

## What it contributes to THIS project
This is the primary behavioral data source for the UNDERGRADUATE level of Employer Reputation (ER) in the AR-ER gap. The Field-of-Study files report post-completion earnings at the exact granularity this project needs: the unique combination of institution (UNITID/OPEID), a 4-digit CIP code, and a credential level (certificate, associate, bachelor, graduate). Because the join key for the entire project is the CIP code, Scorecard supplies the labor-market outcome (earnings) that we rank within field across institutions to build the ER_rank, against which the SpringRank faculty-hiring prestige (AR_rank) is correlated to compute Gap_field = 1 - Spearman(prestige_rank, ER_rank). It also lets us cover non-PhD/undergrad programs that the NSF SDR (PhD-only, SEH-only) cannot, and to contrast credential fields (law, finance) versus near-zero-gap fields (CS).

## Specific equation / result / dataset we reuse
We lift the Field-of-Study earnings columns keyed on (institution ID x 4-digit CIP x credential level): median earnings of graduates measured one year after completion (Title IV completers), plus the 25th/75th earnings percentile fields and median debt at graduation. The pooled two-year award-year cohorts (e.g., pooled 2014-15/2015-16 ... through 2018-19/2019-20) provide the panel structure. Concretely: read the field-of-study ZIP / API, filter to credential = bachelor (undergrad level) and graduate (to cross-check the PhD level), group by 4-digit CIP within field, and rank institutions by median earnings to form ER_rank.
