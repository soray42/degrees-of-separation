# ncses_sdr
**Full citation:** National Science Foundation, National Center for Science and Engineering Statistics (NCSES). Survey of Doctorate Recipients (SDR) and Survey of Earned Doctorates (SED). Alexandria, VA: NSF NCSES. ncses.nsf.gov.
**DOI / URL:** https://ncses.nsf.gov/surveys/doctorate-recipients/ and https://ncses.nsf.gov/surveys/earned-doctorates/
**Access level:** full text (survey documentation and public data tools read directly; restricted-use microdata requires a licensing agreement)

## What it contributes to THIS project
The SDR is the primary behavioral data source for the PhD level of Employer Reputation (ER). It is a biennial longitudinal panel (since 1973) of individuals holding a U.S. research doctorate in science, engineering, or health, and it records exactly the two outcome dimensions this project decomposes into ER: salary for full-time employees and employment SECTOR (academia / industry / government). Because each respondent's field of doctorate is coded, we can rank PhD-granting institutions within field by graduate earnings/sector outcomes to build the PhD-level ER_rank that we Spearman-correlate against the SpringRank faculty-hiring prestige (AR_rank) for Gap_field. The SED (annual census of all U.S. research-doctorate recipients) supplies the Doctorate Records File sampling frame, the field-of-doctorate taxonomy, and the institution-of-doctorate field that lets us map degrees to institutions and, via the CIP join key, align them with Scorecard and O*NET. The academia-vs-industry sector split in the SDR is the empirical anchor for the task-distance hypothesis (CS PhDs flow easily to industry -> near-zero gap; credential fields do not).

## Specific equation / result / dataset we reuse
We reuse the SDR variables: median/annualized salary of full-time employed doctorate holders, employment SECTOR (4-year educational institution vs. business/industry vs. government), field of doctorate, and years-since-degree, all available through the NCSES data explorer / SESTAT public tables and the restricted-use microdata file. From the SED we reuse the field-of-doctorate classification and institution-of-doctorate to construct the within-field institution panel. Operationally: within each doctoral field, rank institutions by their graduates' median salary (and by share entering industry) to form the PhD ER_rank, then compute Gap_field = 1 - Spearman(SpringRank prestige_rank, ER_rank).
